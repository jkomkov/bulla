# Foreign micro-lineage curator packet

Author three hidden micro-lineages from a practice, system, or institution not
designed by the Bulla team. Each lineage contains exactly:

1. one source case and the forum finding actually authorized for it;
2. one follow-on case that should fall under the same explicit reason;
3. one superficially similar foil that the reason must not reach; and
4. one case whose resolution requires a genuinely fresh reason.

For each case, bind the consequential action, disputed term, protected
distinction, evidence policy, governing authority, closure warrant, semantic
epoch, and harm if the classification is wrong. Do not reveal the applicability
labels until the frozen Bulla and independent-checker outputs are committed.

The curator may be compensated and may publish every disagreement. Their
organizational ties, prior Bulla exposure, sources, and redistribution limits
are recorded. External authorship is not represented as incentive independence.
