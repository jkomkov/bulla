# Bring One Seam — intake and return contract

Status: recruitment packet; not external evidence until a foreign participant
submits and receipt-binds an artifact.

## Intake

The intake should take less than fifteen minutes. Do not provide credentials,
personal data, secrets, or material you lack authority to disclose.

1. Which two systems or organizations are involved?
2. What consequential action crosses the seam?
3. Which shared term may mean different things?
4. What distinction must not be silently changed?
5. Which evidence currently exists?
6. Who is authorized to decide the residue?
7. What harm occurs if the interpretation is wrong?

The curator states compensation, organizational ties, prior exposure to the
candidate, publication permissions, and whether the inputs may be redistributed.

## Return

The private report contains the typed claim chain; a countermodel or distinction;
safe RELY and REFUSE regions; residual escalation; permitted evidence options;
non-scalarized disclosure, latency, consent, authority, and monetary burdens;
the finality obstruction; and a replayable receipt. Bulla may return only an
abstention. No report claims legal advice or worldly truth.

The participant may publish disagreement and has no endorsement obligation.
