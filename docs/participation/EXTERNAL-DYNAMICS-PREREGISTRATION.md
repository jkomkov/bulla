accepted until the candidate commit and packet hashes are frozen.

- four foreign curators, three hidden micro-lineages each;
- one source case, valid transfer, superficial foil, and fresh-reason challenge
  per lineage: 48 cases total;
- two blinded applicability ratings for each of 36 follow-ons: 72 primary ratings;
- one independently authored boundary checker;
- one nonlocal shadow integration;
- one hostile privacy/authority review.

Internal identities, synthetic persons, supplied checkers, GitHub runners, and
paid work without disclosed incentive ties do not establish independence.

- `FOREIGN_SIGNAL_PRESENT`: zero agreed unsafe applications; authorized transfer
  beats matched scrambling on at least 9/12 lineages; median marginal yield at
  least two; and independent checker parity.
- `FOREIGN_SIGNAL_LIMITED`: zero agreed unsafe applications with positive but
  sub-threshold reuse.
- `FOREIGN_SIGNAL_ABSENT`: median yield at most one or no dominance on at least
  half the lineages.
- any agreed unsafe application: `SOUNDNESS_FAILURE`.

Unavailable participants remain `BLOCKED`. No internal substitute is permitted.
Security or reproducibility repair after freeze invalidates every affected run.
