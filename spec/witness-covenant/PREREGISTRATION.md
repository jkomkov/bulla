# Witness Covenant 0.1 Preregistration

Frozen question: can a closed promise make one objective witness fault eligible
for bounded recourse without upgrading witness trust, substantive truth, or
capital collectibility?

Primary success conditions:

- Python and standalone Node reports agree on every consequence-bearing field.
- Two authentic, jointly observed, same-log, same-epoch, same-size heads with
  different roots establish the covered fault.
- Either head alone cannot establish the fault.
- A different operator, log, epoch, or tree size does not satisfy the predicate.
- An open challenge prevents remedy eligibility.
- Eligibility does not issue authorization; authorization does not establish
  execution or collection.
- Adding or removing the reported bond changes only capital and recourse fields.
- A witnessed model-identity claim remains challenge-required.

Kill or narrow the claim if packet-carried trust can authenticate a checkpoint,
one view can establish equivocation, a nominal bond changes trust or truth, an
uncovered claim reaches automatic recourse, or removed evidence improves a
protected conclusion.

Canonical scenarios are `consistent`, `fork-open`, `fork-closed`,
`fork-authorized`, and `model-dispute`. Expected reports are generated and
committed before protected behavior is changed.
