# Retained inference evidence comprehension gate

The frozen protocol is `comprehension-protocol.json`; its detached SHA-256 is
`comprehension-protocol.json.sha256`. Version 0.2 supersedes the unused
version 0.1 instrument before any gate was opened or reader was contacted. The
v0.1 digest remains recorded in the v0.2 lineage.

No person may be contacted until a gate-opening record binds the v0.2 digest to
the exact preview commit, immutable route, generated command block, and
reproduction kit.

Five responses are qualifying product-comprehension evidence, not external
verification of the inference-clearing profile. Participants remain
pseudonymous in the repository. First responses are retained verbatim and
scored against the frozen rubric. Navigation and accessibility help are the
only permitted assistance.

Each reader record stores the provider-exit recheck and the receipt-boundary
challenge as separate outcomes. The browser-interaction requirement passes for
a reader only when both are completed without semantic assistance.

The official gate is single-shot. A failing attempt records
`NOT_ESTABLISHED` and keeps the pull requests draft. A corrected candidate
requires a new gate opening and five fresh eligible readers; it cannot reuse
the failed candidate's evidence.

This directory intentionally contains no gate opening, participant response,
or result record yet. Their absence means the human gate remains `BLOCKED`.

Future evidence is scored with `score_comprehension_gate.py`. The scorer takes
the coordinator trust context from a separate path, authenticates the gate,
attempt manifest, and dual-signed reader records, recomputes response and page
commitments, enforces participant topology, and derives the dimensional result.
