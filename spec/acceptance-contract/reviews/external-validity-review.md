# Acceptance Contract alpha — external-validity review

**Tier:** R3  
**Scope:** prior-art boundary, product claim, conditional evidence language,
inspectability, operational failure modes, and suppressed conclusions.

## Material findings and corrections

1. **A missing record is not an exact cure.** The result now says what a newly
   supplied and verified record would need to establish. It does not say the
   record exists, will report `PASS`, minimizes cost or disclosure, or guarantees
   promotion. Minimality is explicitly limited to the finite declared catalog.
2. **Eligibility was too easy to read as deployment.** Every outcome reports
   authorization and execution separately. Even `PROCEED / ELIGIBLE` leaves
   authorization `NOT_ISSUED` and execution `NOT_ATTEMPTED`.
3. **The alpha could be mistaken for a new policy engine.** The prior-art matrix
   now credits policy engines, authorization systems, workflow gates,
   supply-chain attestations, provenance systems, and counterfactual repair.
   The claimed contribution is the bounded composition around a retained
   counterparty transaction.

## Disposition

`PASS` for a source-only synthetic alpha. Worldly truth, historical execution,
denominator completeness, legal enforceability, and external validation remain
unestablished and are stated in every report.
